  • Performers will not travel to or arrive at the venue unless the booking has been paid in full.
  • Unpaid dates may be offered to other clients.

Payment reserves our performers, their time, and the agreed services for your event.

5. Extra Performance Time

We understand that sometimes the party is going so well that nobody wants the entertainment to end!

We are happy to consider extending the experience when possible.

However:

  • Extra performance time is not automatically included.
  • Extensions depend on performer availability and safety.
  • Additional time is charged in 30-minute increments at the extension rate agreed with the client.
  • Extensions must be approved and paid for before the additional performance time begins.
  • Performers may decline extensions because of other commitments, required breaks, or safety concerns.

If an extension is not approved and paid for, the performance will end at the originally agreed time.

6. Arrival, Delays & Waiting Time

AfroRitmara performers will arrive at least 15 minutes before the scheduled start time, or earlier if additional setup has been agreed upon.

The client must ensure the venue is ready and accessible at the agreed time.

If an event starts late because of the client, venue, or other circumstances outside AfroRitmara's control:

  • The original performance ending time still applies.
  • Waiting time counts toward the reserved booking period.
  • A delayed start does not automatically extend the performance.
  • Additional time may be provided only if available, agreed upon, and paid for.

AfroRitmara will make reasonable efforts to accommodate delays, but we cannot guarantee additional time without charge.

7. Drinking Water, Breaks & Performer Facilities

Providing a safe and comfortable working environment helps our performers deliver their best.

The client or venue agrees to provide:

  • Safe drinking water for every performer throughout their time at the venue.
  • At least 15 minutes of break time for every hour of performance activity.
  • A safe, clean performance area with suitable flooring.
  • Reliable electricity and working power connections.
  • A suitable sound system, unless otherwise agreed.
  • A private or appropriate area for changing, preparation, and belongings.
  • Access to the venue before the performance.
  • A designated contact person to coordinate the event.

Drinking water is a required part of performer welfare, not an optional courtesy.

If drinking water is not provided, AfroRitmara may obtain it and charge the client the reasonable actual cost.

If essential facilities or safe conditions are unavailable, the performance may be delayed or paused until the issue is resolved.

AfroRitmara is not required to provide additional performance time without charge to compensate for delays caused by missing client-provided facilities.

8. Cancellations & Rescheduling

We understand that plans sometimes change. However, when you reserve a performance, we commit performers and decline other opportunities for that time.

All cancellations and rescheduling requests must be made in writing through WhatsApp or email.

Cancellation or Rescheduling Credits

14 or More Calendar Days Before the Performance

You may request one change to another available performance date.

Up to 100% of your original booking payment may be transferred as credit toward the rescheduled performance, subject to the agreed booking scope.

No additional rescheduling charge applies to the first approved date change.

7–13 Calendar Days Before the Performance

You may receive a credit equal to 50% of the original performance fee toward another available booking.

The remaining 50% is retained as a cancellation charge for reserving the performers and date.

Fewer Than 7 Calendar Days Before the Performance

No rescheduling credit is provided.

The booking payment is retained as the agreed late-cancellation charge, subject to applicable law.

Credit Rules

  • Credits are not cash refunds.
  • Credits may be used toward another AfroRitmara performance.
  • Credits expire six months after the original performance date.
  • Unused credits have no value after that period.
  • Rescheduled performances depend on performer availability.
  • If the new performance costs more, the client must pay the difference in full before confirmation.
  • If the new performance costs less, any unused eligible credit remains available until its original expiration date.
  • Additional date changes may be subject to the cancellation rules in effect when the change is requested.

These rules apply to client-requested cancellations and rescheduling. They do not limit any refund or other remedy required by applicable law.

Special Rule for Weekly Entertainment Bookings

For regular weekly entertainment arrangements, a client may request that an affected performance be moved by up to seven calendar days, subject to performer availability and written approval.

This is a rescheduling arrangement, not an additional free performance.

If the requested change falls within seven days of the original performance, the normal late-rescheduling credit rules apply unless AfroRitmara agrees otherwise in writing.

If approved, the new performance date must be confirmed in writing.

If AfroRitmara Cancels

If AfroRitmara cannot provide the agreed performance, we will offer a suitable alternative date or refund the amount paid for the services we cannot deliver.

Clients will not lose payment because of an AfroRitmara cancellation.
