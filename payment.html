02 / How to pay

Four simple steps.

  1. Tap Copy Phone Number to save 024 303 3213 to your clipboard.
  2. Tap the applicable MTN dialer button. If your phone only fills in the code, tap Call to open the MTN menu. Follow the prompts to pay and paste the number when asked.
  3. Enter the agreed amount and include your venue/event and service as the reference.
  4. Check the displayed recipient name, approve the payment securely, and keep your confirmation.

After paying, send your venue name, event date and transaction ID to the AfroRitmara team so we can match your payment to the booking.

Never share your Mobile Money PIN with us or anyone else.

03 / Venue readiness

Prepare the space. We'll bring the energy.

A well-prepared venue protects performers, keeps your event running smoothly and gives guests the best possible experience.

01 / Hydration

Drinking water

Provide safe drinking water for all performers, available before, during and after the performance.

02 / Performance area

Clear & safe floor

Provide a clean, dry, stable, non-slip surface with enough space to move safely. Remove trip hazards, cables and sharp objects.

03 / Sound & power

Ready to perform

Provide functional sound equipment, the agreed music connection and safe, dependable electrical access.

04 / Timing

Arrival & breaks

Plan for performers to arrive about 15 minutes early and allow 15 minutes of rest for each hour of performance.

05 / Respect

Professional boundaries

Help maintain a respectful environment. Guests should follow performers' instructions and respect physical boundaries.

06 / Conditions

Comfort & access

Arrange safe access, reasonable ventilation, nearby washrooms and a place for performers to store belongings.

Please flag any special conditions early: outdoor stages, uneven surfaces, limited space, unreliable power, audience interaction plans or weather exposure. Any event-specific arrangements should be agreed with AfroRitmara before the performance.
